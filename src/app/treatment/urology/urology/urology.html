What is Urology?

Urology is about taking care of people who have problems with their bodies. It helps with surgical treatments for conditions that affect the Kidneys. It also affects the Urinary bladder and the Ureters and the Urethra and the Prostate gland and the Male reproductive organs.

  • Kidneys

Common Urological Conditions Treated

Kidney Stones

Kidney stones are hard mineral deposits formed in the kidneys. Symptoms may include severe pain, blood in urine, nausea, and difficulty urinating.

Enlarged Prostate (BPH)

Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH) is common in older men and can cause urinary problems such as frequent urination and weak urine flow.

Urinary Tract Infections (UTI)

UTIs affect the urinary system and may cause pain during urination, fever, and frequent urge to urinate.

Prostate Cancer

Prostate cancer develops in the prostate gland and requires timely diagnosis and treatment for better outcomes.

Bladder Cancer

Bladder cancer occurs when abnormal cells grow in the bladder lining.

Kidney Cancer

Kidney cancer affects kidney tissues and may require surgery or targeted therapy.

Male Infertility

Urologists also treat male infertility caused by low sperm count, varicocele, or hormonal problems.

Erectile Dysfunction

This condition affects a man's ability to achieve or maintain an erection.

Urinary Incontinence

Loss of bladder control can affect both men and women and may require medication or surgery.

Symptoms That Require A Urology Consultation

You should go see a urologist if you have symptoms like these.

  • Blood in your urine is a problem
  • Pain when you urinate is not normal
  • You have to urinate very often
  • You have kidney stone pain which can be very bad
  • It is hard for you to pass urine
  • You have pain, in your lower abdomen
  • Men may have infertility issues that need to be checked by a urologist
  • Men can also have erectile dysfunction which is a urology problem
  • You have urinary leakage which is very embarrassing and needs to be treated by a urologist.
