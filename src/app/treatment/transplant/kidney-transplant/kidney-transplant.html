What is Kidney Transplantation?

A kidney transplant is a surgery that saves lives by replacing a damaged or failed kidney with an one from a donor. Doctors usually suggest a kidney transplant, to patients who have bad kidney disease or when their kidneys fail and dialysis is not working well for long. When a kidney transplant is successful it can help patients live an active life be more independent and be healthier.

When is a Kidney Transplant Needed?

Doctors may think a transplant is an idea for a patient who has certain health problems. These problems include:

  • kidney disease that is very bad
  • Kidney failure that means the patient needs dialysis for a long time
  • Kidney damage from diabetes that is very severe
  • Kidney problems that're genetic
  • High blood pressure that is not controlled and is hurting the kidneys.

A transplant is often a choice than dialysis for the rest of the patients life because a transplant can make the patients life better and help the patient live longer. A kidney transplant can offer a patient a life and help the patient live longer than kidney dialysis can. Doctors think a kidney transplant is an option, for patients who have these kidney problems.

Types of Kidney Transplant

Living Donor Transplant

When it comes to a Living Donor Transplant, a person, usually a family member donates one of their kidneys. The Kidney Transplant from a Living Donor usually has some benefits.

These benefits include:

  • Shorter waiting time for the Kidney Transplant
  • Better success rates for the Kidney Transplant
  • Faster recovery, from the Kidney Transplant

Deceased Donor Transplant

Here a person who has passed away donates their kidney. Only if they had agreed to organ donation before they died. Patients who need a kidney are put on a waiting list. They wait until a suitable kidney is available, for them.

How the Procedure Works

The surgery involves putting the kidney in the lower abdomen. The new kidney is then connected to the blood vessels and the bladder. The old kidneys are usually not removed unless there is a medical reason to take them out. The new kidney usually starts working within a days.

Recovery After Transplant

Patients usually stay in the hospital for a week. They need to go to the hospital for regular check ups. The doctors give them medicines called immunosuppressant medicines. These medicines help the body get used to the kidney. With the care many people have a kidney transplant that works well for a long time. The new kidney functions well for years.

Benefits of Kidney Transplant

  • Freedom from dialysis
  • People have energy and they are healthier
  • They can eat better. Sleep better
  • They can live for a time
  • They can do their daily activities again
