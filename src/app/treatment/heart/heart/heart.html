Cardiac surgery in India

Cardiac surgery is a type of surgery that doctors do to help people with heart problems. They do this surgery when the person cannot get better by taking medicine or changing the way they live.

Cardiac surgery can really help people because it makes the blood flow better fixes the heart so it works properly and stops bad things from happening to the person.

The main goal of surgery is to make the heart work better and cardiac surgery is used to treat diseases of the heart and blood vessels.

Conditions Treated with Cardiac Surgery

Cardiac surgery may be required for:

  • Coronary artery disease (blocked heart arteries)
  • Heart valve disease
  • Congenital heart defects
  • Heart failure
  • Aortic aneurysm or dissection
  • Irregular heart rhythm conditions

Types of Cardiac Surgeries

Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)

The Coronary Artery Bypass Graft or bypass surgery helps the heart get blood by making a new path, around the arteries that are blocked.

Bypass surgery is one of the heart surgeries that doctors do a lot and it really works well to stop chest pain make the heart work better and lower the risk of having a heart attack.

Heart Valve Repair or Replacement

This surgery is for heart valves that are damaged or too narrow which affects the flow of blood. Surgeons can. Fix the valve or replace it with a new one, either mechanical or biological depending on what is wrong, with it.

It helps get the blood flowing again and stops the heart from failing.

Minimally Invasive Cardiac Surgery

Some heart operations can now be done with cuts instead of cutting open the whole chest.

  • The good things about this are
  • The patient will feel pain
  • The marks on the skin will be really small
  • The patient does not have to stay in the hospital for a time
  • The patient can get back, to life faster

This way of doing heart operations is used for certain heart valve surgeries, heart bypass procedures and problems that people are born with like congenital defects.

Congenital Heart Surgery

This surgery fixes heart problems that people are born with like holes, in the heart walls or bad heart valves.

The surgery can be done on kids or adults it just depends on when the heart problem's found.

Aortic Surgery

The aortic surgery is done to fix problems with the aorta. The aorta is the artery that carries blood from the heart.

It can get too big. It can tear or get blocked. The aortic surgery helps stop bad things from happening.

These bad things can be the aorta breaking open or the blood not getting to the parts of the body. The aortic surgery is very important, for the aorta. It helps keep people safe.

Heart Transplant Surgery

When people have bad heart failure and other treatments do not work a heart transplant might be the best option.

The old heart that is not working well is taken out. A healthy heart, from a donor is put in its place to make the heart work properly again and help the person live longer.

Benefits of Modern Cardiac Surgery
  • Advanced surgical technology and monitoring
  • High success rates with experienced surgeons
  • Improved blood circulation and heart function
  • Relief from chest pain and breathlessness
  • Better quality of life and long-term survival
