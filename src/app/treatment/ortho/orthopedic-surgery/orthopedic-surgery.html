Orthopaedic Surgery

Orthopaedic surgery is about figuring out and fixing problems with the bones, joints, ligaments, tendons and muscles. You should think about surgery when other things like medicine, physiotherapy or changing your lifestyle do not help with the pain or get you moving again. New orthopaedic surgery methods help people with issues get moving feel less pain and have a better life. Orthopaedic surgery can really make a difference for people who have problems, with their bones, joints, ligaments, tendons and muscles.

Conditions Treated with Orthopaedic Surgery

Orthopaedic surgery may be required for:

  • Severe arthritis (knee, hip, shoulder)
  • Fractures and bone injuries
  • Sports injuries (ligament or tendon tears)
  • Spine disorders and back pain
  • Joint deformities or instability
  • Osteoporosis-related fractures
  • Bone infections or tumors

Types of Orthopaedic Surgeries

Joint Replacement Surgery

Joint replacement is something that a lot of people have to get. This is because they have joints that need to be fixed. So doctors put in joints to help people move around and feel less pain.

Some of the joints that people get replaced are:

  • Knee replacement
  • Hip replacement
  • Shoulder replacement

Joint replacement is really good at helping people. It can make them feel better for a very long time. Joint replacement is a way to get rid of pain and it can really help people, with bad joints like knee replacement or hip replacement or shoulder replacement.

Arthroscopic Surgery

Arthroscopy is a surgery that uses a small camera and tiny tools. It is often used to fix:

  • Torn ligaments like ACL and PCL
  • Meniscus tears
  • Injuries to the shoulder
  • Damage to the cartilage

The benefits of arthroscopy include cuts, less pain and getting back, to normal quickly.

Fracture Repair Surgery

When bones are really badly broken the doctor will need to do surgery to fix the bones and make them stable again using things, like plates, screws, rods or pins. This helps the bones heal properly and makes sure the bones work like they are supposed to.

Spine Surgery

Orthopaedic spine surgery helps with problems like slipped disc, spinal stenosis and spine deformities. Some of the procedures used are decompression surgery, fusion and simple keyhole spine surgeries to take the pressure, off nerves and make the spine more stable.

Sports Injury Surgery

Orthopaedic surgeons help people who get hurt when they are active. They fix problems like torn ligaments and damaged tendons. Sometimes peoples joints are not stable. That is a problem too. When orthopaedic surgeons do surgery to fix these problems it helps athletes and people who are active get back, to moving like they used to.

Benefits of Modern Orthopaedic Surgery

  • Advanced minimally invasive techniques
  • Faster recovery time
  • Improved joint function and mobility
  • Reduced pain and disability
  • High success and long-lasting results

Orthopaedic Surgery FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about orthopaedic procedures, implants, recovery, and travel support in India.

What orthopaedic procedures are most commonly performed in India for international patients?

The most sought-after procedures include total knee replacement, hip replacement, shoulder arthroplasty, ACL reconstruction, arthroscopic joint surgery, and complex limb deformity corrections, all performed at internationally benchmarked standards.

What implant brands are used? Are they internationally certified?

India's top orthopaedic hospitals use globally recognised implant brands such as Zimmer Biomet, Stryker, DePuy Synthes, and Smith & Nephew, all FDA and CE certified. Your surgeon recommends the best fit for your age, weight, and activity level.

Is computer-assisted or robotic joint replacement available in India?

Yes. Leading Indian hospitals use robotic systems like MAKO and Stryker for knee and hip replacements, enabling highly precise implant positioning, less tissue damage, and faster recovery.

How do I send my X-rays and reports for an orthopaedic opinion before travelling?

You can share digital copies of your X-rays, MRI scans, and medical history through our online portal or via WhatsApp/email. Our orthopaedic specialist reviews them and provides a treatment recommendation within 48 hours.

What is the typical recovery timeline for knee or hip replacement in India?

Most patients are walking with support within 24-48 hours of surgery. Hospital stay is usually 4-5 days, followed by 2 weeks of in-country physiotherapy before flying home. Full recovery to normal activity takes 6-12 weeks.

Will physiotherapy and rehabilitation be provided during my stay in India?

Yes. In-hospital physiotherapy begins the day after surgery. Before discharge, your physiotherapist also trains you and your caregiver on exercises to continue at home, along with a detailed written rehabilitation plan.

What precautions should I take during the flight back home after orthopaedic surgery?

Your surgeon provides specific travel guidelines, including advice on compression stockings, leg movement exercises, and medication to prevent blood clots during long flights. We also arrange wheelchair assistance at the airport if needed.
